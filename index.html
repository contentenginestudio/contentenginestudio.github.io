About the Content Engine app

Content Engine is a private production tool we built for our own channels. It is not a public service: there are no sign-ups, and nobody outside our team can use it. It runs on the owner's computer and connects only to accounts we own and control.

With our authorization, it uses each platform's official API to:

  • YouTube (YouTube API Services): upload our videos, set their titles, descriptions, thumbnails and playlists, and read the analytics for our own channels.
  • TikTok (planned): post our videos to our own TikTok accounts, or send them to our drafts.
  • Facebook and Instagram (planned): publish our videos to our own Facebook Pages and Instagram professional accounts, and read their insights.

We use the statistics only to see how our own videos perform and to decide what to make next. Content Engine does not collect information about viewers, never sells data, and does not use platform data for advertising or to train AI models.
